# Appearance

Same Slate preserves the Baloo 2 and Inter fonts, familiar controls, responsive layouts and audio from Google Feud. It uses a deep teal stage, forest-green panels and pop-ups, turquoise accents, warm gold confetti and bright slate cards. Emerald gradients, mint borders and darker green form fields keep menus, player cards and Hall of Fame panels distinct from Google Feud in both standard and night mode. The background, sharing card and app icon are original generated artwork. Exact briefs are in `artwork-manifest.json`. The footer ends with Created by Sean.
